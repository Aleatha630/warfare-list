
# ⚔️ WARFARE ENCYCLOPEDIA

**91,500 entries. 91 sections. From ancient battlefields to battles that haven't been invented yet.**

A giant, searchable catalog of warfare terminology, historical combat traditions, strategic competition, military environments, emerging technology, meme culture, and speculative worlds. One encyclopedia. No hunting through dozens of separate lists.

## Get straight to it

**Open `warfare_encyclopedia_91500_interactive.html` in your browser.** That's the explorer: search by term or entry number, filter by section or subject, and page through results. It works **offline**, without a server, installation, or external libraries.

Prefer reading or working with the data? The complete encyclopedia is in `warfare_encyclopedia_91500.md` and `.txt`. The 90,000 new profiles are also in `warfare_new_90000.csv`.

## What's inside

- **Ancient → modern:** infantry, fortifications, cavalry, gunpowder, mechanized forces, air and naval warfare.
- **Where wars happen:** cities, mountains, jungles, deserts, polar regions, coastlines, oceans, and underground spaces.
- **How conflicts unfold:** strategies, doctrines, defenses, logistics, intelligence, political rivalry, economic pressure, and information contests.
- **Emerging frontiers:** drones, robotics, artificial intelligence, cyber systems, satellites, and electromagnetic competition.
- **Beyond reality:** interstellar civilizations, orbital habitats, magic, multiverses, simulations, and other imagined conflicts.
- **Meme warfare:** a dedicated section for internet humor, online rivalries, and meme-based cultural competition—not the focus of the whole project.

### Yes, meme warfare has its own section.

**Section 57 · Entries #1,351–1,400** contains 50 playful meme-warfare categories. Everything else gets room to breathe, too.

## How 91,500 entries work

**#1–1,500:** The original mixed glossary of established concepts, descriptive labels, informal categories, and fictional ideas.

**#1,501–91,500:** Thirty new sections containing **3,000 profiles each**, built from **10 approaches × 15 settings × 20 objectives** per section. Each numbered combination has a distinct title and description.

It's a reference *and* a worldbuilding classification system. The generated profiles are original combinations, not 90,000 separate doctrines officially recognized by armed forces.
